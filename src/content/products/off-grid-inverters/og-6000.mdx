The **OG-6000** is the workhorse of our off-grid line: 6 kW continuous,
12 kW surge, and enough charging throughput to run a whole household or
workshop far from any power line.

## Handles real loads

Motor start is the hardest job an off-grid inverter faces. The OG-6000's
12000 W surge capacity starts air conditioners, pumps and compressors that
trip lesser units. Three-stage smart charging and temperature compensation
get the longest possible life from lead-acid banks, while LiFePO4 protocols
are supported natively over RS485.
