The **OG-3500** bundles an inverter and charge controller into one box —
the simplest way to build a standalone power system where the grid doesn't
reach. One device, one installation, one warranty.

## Built for remote sites

Pure sine wave output runs pumps, fridges and telecom equipment safely, while
the generator input with auto-start signal keeps batteries charged through
long cloudy periods. The LCD panel shows battery voltage, load percentage and
fault history without needing any app or connectivity.
