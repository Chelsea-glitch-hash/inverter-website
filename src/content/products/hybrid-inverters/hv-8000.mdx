The **HV-8000** extends our hybrid platform to 8 kW, covering large homes,
farmsteads and small commercial sites that need serious storage throughput.
With 120 A of battery charging current, a full 48 V battery bank can be
recharged from solar within a single sunny day.

## One platform, many configurations

The HV-8000 shares its control platform with the HV-5000 — same monitoring
app, same battery protocol library, same installation workflow. Installers
standardize on one product family across residential and light-commercial
projects, and distributors hold fewer SKUs in stock.

## Grid-code flexibility

Firmware grid profiles for major markets are configurable at production time —
voltage/frequency windows, export limits and anti-islanding behavior. For OEM
customers we validate and pre-load your market's requirements on the line.
