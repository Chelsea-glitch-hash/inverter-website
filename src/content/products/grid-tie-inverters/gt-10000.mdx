The **GT-10000** brings commercial-grade engineering to the 10 kW class:
a 1000 VDC input architecture that cuts DC cabling losses, IP66 protection
for harsh environments, and grid-support functions required by modern
commercial interconnection standards.

## Designed for C&I projects

Three-phase output, two MPPT trackers with two strings each, and reactive
power control make the GT-10000 a straightforward fit for office rooftops,
factory buildings and carport arrays. Plant developers can integrate it via
RS485 or Ethernet into existing SCADA systems.
