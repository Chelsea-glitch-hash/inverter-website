The **GT-3000** is our entry into residential grid-tied systems: a light,
fanless string inverter that prioritizes yield and reliability over feature
bloat. Two MPPT trackers handle split-orientation rooftops, and the 9.5 kg
housing makes it a genuine one-person installation.

## Maximized yield, minimized maintenance

Natural convection cooling removes the most common field failure point —
fans — entirely. Combined with IP65 sealing and surge protection on both DC
and AC sides, the GT-3000 is designed for a 10+ year service life with zero
scheduled maintenance.
